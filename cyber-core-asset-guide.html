1. 공통 프롬프트

Create a production-ready 2D game asset concept sheet for [SUBJECT].

Top-down three-quarter view, cyber intrusion survivorlike game, bold readable silhouette, minimal geometric cyberpunk design, clean vector-like rendering, controlled neon glow, dark internal shadows, no text, no logo, no UI frame.

The asset must remain recognizable at 48–64 pixels.
Use clear shape language and avoid excessive tiny details.
Neutral transparent background, evenly spaced concept sheet.

플레이어·침투 진영

Intruder faction design language: asymmetrical triangular forms, saturated magenta, violet or lime accents, flowing glitch trails, fast agile appearance.

적·보안 진영

Security faction design language: rigid symmetry, white and pale cyan armor, square and shield-based construction, hostile orange warning lights.

노드 공통 구조

Shared node structure: hexagonal floor platform, neutral gunmetal chassis, circular capture boundary, central holographic device, clear top-down readability.

무기·VFX 공통 구조

Focus on projectile shape, trail, impact effect, telegraph boundary, and attack readability. Avoid poster composition and character-focused illustration.
사용법: 아래 카드의 “전체 프롬프트 복사”를 누르면 해당 에셋 전용 요구사항이 통째로 복사된다. 초안은 대상당 3안만 뽑고 48px 축소 테스트에서 가장 잘 읽히는 안을 남겨라.
